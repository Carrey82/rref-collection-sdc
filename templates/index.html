We chase the promise, not the invoice

A small supplier loses money — but not because it cannot write a stern email. It loses money because nobody remembers what the customer promised. {{app}} keeps that promise on record, drafts the message only when the promise is gone — and stays quiet when it is still good.

What the restraint is worth

≈ 12 min per chase

Two costs, both real. A manual reminder is ~12 minutes of finding records and typing — a reviewed draft is ~1 minute. And a badly timed chase costs a customer: {{held_clause}} still inside a date the customer promised, so the system drafts nothing for them. That is what restraint is worth — the chase that never went out, and the twelve minutes nobody had to spend.
