Why not let the model decide who to chase?

Same input, same output rules are testable
Explained to a customer in one sentence "38 days late, twice promised"
If it is wrong, who is liable? the supplier, not the model

There are 23 unit tests on the rule layer and none on the wording layer, on purpose: the rule layer decides money, the wording layer decides politeness.

Hard limits in the system prompt

    {{rules}}

The prompt says use only the facts given. The dates in a draft are quoted from the promise ledger, so a draft cannot promise something the customer never said.

Three things it can never do

Send. Approval is a click on the review page, logged with a name.
Pick the customer. Priority is amount × √days × broken promises.
Invent a date. Dates only enter through the promise ledger, typed by a person who heard them.

Turning the model on

Three environment variables — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. If the call fails, times out, or returns something short, the template takes over and the demo keeps working:

LLM_BASE_URL=https://api.openai.com/v1
LLM_API_KEY=sk-...
LLM_MODEL=gpt-4o-mini
